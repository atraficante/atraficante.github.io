# Start Here


### Make sure you have these required documents
- Photo ID (I assume you guys all have a driver's license…)
- FAA medical certificate (if you do not have one, find an aviation medical examiner [here](https://www.faa.gov/pilots/amelocator) and apply for a first class if able)
- Student pilot’s license (if you do not have one apply for one [here](https://iacra.faa.gov/IACRA/Default.aspx), then send me the FTN number the application generates)
- An endorsement from me (which you will get when you are ready to solo)

Once the administrative items are in progress, start studying! Below are some links that I find useful.

## Educational Stuff
- [PPL/CPL](./lesson-plans/) and [IFR](./lesson-plans/instrumentIndex) Lesson Plans - I wrote these to help me review for CFI, hopefully they are useful!
- [ERAU Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/@ERAUSpecialVFR/videos) - ERAU has some excellent visuals in their videos and is my favorite youtube channel for educational flying videos. Sort their videos by most popular.
- [Flight Insight Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/@flightinsight9111) - this guy has an ability to take complex topics and explain them super clearly
- [Boldmethod](https://www.boldmethod.com/) - check out the “CFI Tools” page or just read some articles

## FAA References
- [Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (PHAK)](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/phak) - for those that learn best by reading 
- [Airplane Flying Handbook](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/airplane_handbook) - a great dive into manuevers in a textbook format 
- [ECFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/) - online FAR/AIM…type 14 CFR X.X to find any regulation (for example, 14 CFR 61.51) 
- [Private Pilot Airmen Certification Standards](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf) - Essentially your PPL study guide straight from the FAA! READ THIS!
