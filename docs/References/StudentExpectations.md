# Student Expectations

# Student Pilots

## Stage 1 (Pre-Solo)
- Perform an unassisted preflight
- Lookup frequencies and communicate with the appropriate people when transiting to Fox, Mojave, Rosamond or North Base
- Perform a takeoff briefing (normal takeoff and emergency procedures)
	- Engine failure during takeoff roll or with runway remaining
	- Engine failure after departure or with no runway remaining to stop
	- Engine failure above a certain altitude
	- "Any problem before rotation, power idle, max back pressure, max braking, stop on the runway head of us."
	- "Any problem with runway remaining, land on the remaining runway."
	- "Any problem with no runway remaining, pick a point within 30 degrees, pitch for best glide and prepare for a power off landing. That will involve opening the doors, mixture idle, fuel off, mags and master off."
	- "If we are above 1000 ft and only if we are above 1000 ft will we attempt a turn back to the runway."
	- "Any questions?"
- Perform a normal takeoff with proper callouts
	- Runway verified
	- Takeoff power set
	- Engine gauges in the green
	- Airspeed alive
- Perform a normal landing with no side-loading
- Understand spin recovery

## Stage 2 (Post-Solo)
- Perform a crosswind landing with no side-loading
- Lookup frequencies and communicate with the appropriate people on a cross country
- Generate a cross country flight plan
	- Utilize fuel, time, and distance to climb/descend
	- Utilize cruise performance tables and determine an appropriate power setting
	- Consider NWKRAFT and other preflight actions
- Divert to another airport while on a cross country
- Apply the PAVE checklist before flying
	- P: IMSAFE, currency, proficiency
	- A: AVIATES, performance, capabilities
	- V: Weather, terrain, obstacles
	- E: External pressures
